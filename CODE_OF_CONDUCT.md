# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is trying to help. Argue about the code, not the person behind it.

This project is maintained in spare time. Nobody is on call or owes anyone a fix, and a maintainer
who says "not now" or "won't do" is answering, not being rude.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks, or discriminatory language.
- Unwelcome attention or comments about someone's experience, English, or contribution quality.
- Publishing private information, credentials, device data, account details, logs, or captures.
- Demanding, entitled, or repeated pressure on maintainers.

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, commits, discussions, Discord, and anywhere someone acts as
part of this project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions that do not fit these expectations. Repeated or
severe behavior may result in a block. Report conduct concerns privately to a maintainer; reports are
handled discreetly.
